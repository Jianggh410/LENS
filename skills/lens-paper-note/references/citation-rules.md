# Citation Rules

Use a two-line YAML block scalar and the first author:

```yaml
citation: |-
  First author et al., Journal (Year)
  First author et al., Title, Journal (Year)
```

- Use the first author's displayed name, not the corresponding author, unless they are the same person.
- The first line is the short citation.
- The second line is the long citation containing the paper title.
- Preserve verified journal and publication year.
- Do not invent author, title, journal, or year.
