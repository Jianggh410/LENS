# Figure Rules

Generate the structured Article or Review note before extracting figures.

- Extract numbered Figure headings under `# Results` for Articles. Under Review `# Evidence`, extract both numbered Figure and Table headings when `LENS_REVIEW_EXTRACT_TABLES=1`.
- In Review notes, organize `# Evidence` by the paper's main numbered Figures and Tables in source order: create one `## Figure N. Original English title` or `## Table N. Original English title` subsection per item. Explain Figures through their framework, viewpoint, evidence basis, and author framing; explain Tables through their comparison dimensions, important rows/columns, evidence provenance, and synthesis boundary.
- Match `Fig. N`, `Figure N`, and—for Reviews—`Table N`, including common punctuation after the number. Treat type plus number as the unique identity so Figure 1 and Table 1 never collide.
- Use the original PDF and caption coordinates; never infer an image from the note text alone.
- By default, retain the complete original caption/title and all footnotes together with the Figure or Table whenever they are on the same page and separable as one rectangle. Read `LENS_FIGURE_INCLUDE_ORIGINAL_CAPTION` and `LENS_FIGURE_CAPTION_PADDING_PT` from runtime configuration; defaults are enabled and 6 pt. Include the caption/title through its final line and, for Tables, include every row plus symbol/abbreviation footnotes without admitting unrelated prose or the page footer.
- When a visual item and its caption/title are not on the same page, do not enlarge the crop across page boundaries. Save the visual item alone, record `caption_included: false` and `caption_status: external-not-co-located` in `figures.json`, and retain the managed short title plus Chinese explanation in the note.
- Evaluate the page above the caption, the caption page, and the page after a bottom-page caption. When the caption page is prose-heavy and the following page is visually dense, treat the following page as the Figure page.
- Detect multi-column wrapped-caption layouts used by Science and similar journals. A caption confined to one column must not impose a page-wide horizontal crop boundary; include panels beside and below the caption. When a figure and caption occupy a subset of page columns while article prose continues in an adjacent column, crop only the figure/caption column span.
- For Article notes with a one-column caption, detect a Figure immediately above that caption when the neighboring column at the same height is prose rather than another Figure panel. Crop the Figure and its complete caption column together while excluding the neighboring prose column.
- For Review notes, enable column-aware same-page detection with `LENS_REVIEW_HORIZONTAL_ADJACENCY`. Evaluate Figure-like material both above the caption inside its own column and horizontally beside a narrow caption; reject neighboring regions dominated by prose.
- For all journal layouts, derive the top boundary from the detected running-header boundary and the first Figure drawing, image, or short text element after it (or after preceding prose); retain a 10 pt safety margin above that element. Do not use a fixed `top=55 pt` rule.
- Detect the journal's main page-content frame from a running-header frame/rule or the body-text envelope. Clamp the Figure's left and right crop edges to this frame so bleed marks, journal logos, header decorations, and excess outer margins are not included.
- Read `LENS_FIGURE_TOP_SAFETY_MARGIN`, `LENS_FIGURE_CLAMP_TO_MAIN_FRAME`, `LENS_FIGURE_EDGE_CLEARANCE`, and `LENS_FIGURE_AI_BBOX_PADDING_PT` from the runtime configuration; defaults are 10 pt, enabled, 1 pt, and 10 pt respectively.
- For Cell-style figures whose legend continues on the following page, prefer the preceding visually dense page carrying `legend on next page` over a visually dense following page; this prevents adjacent Figure number swaps.
- Check every initial crop edge for text, drawings, or embedded images that physically touch it. Initial edge contact lowers confidence and forces the complete-page vision-AI verification path instead of direct insertion.
- Parse explicit panel references such as `(A)`–`(J)`, Nature-style `a,`–`j,`, grouped labels such as `c,d,`, and ranges such as `a–d,` from the complete caption. Merge horizontally adjacent caption text blocks before panel parsing so a two-column caption is not truncated after its first column. For Review notes, additionally read the styles configured by `LENS_REVIEW_PANEL_LABEL_STYLES`, including `a |`, `b |`, and `a.`. Require a delimiter after the Figure number so in-text references such as `Fig. 3b` are not mistaken for captions. Verify that the chosen region covers all visually occupied panel regions. Record `expected_panels`, `panel_check`, `caption_included`, `caption_status`, and detected layout metadata in `figures.json`.
- If deterministic geometry cannot verify completeness or the wrapped layout, first save a complete usable region/page candidate and ask the configured vision AI to isolate the target Figure or Table from that candidate.
- Accept an AI-refined crop only when the model returns an in-bounds plausible pixel bounding box. For a Figure, it must verify every panel named by the caption, the complete Figure and caption, intact text/axes/graphics, and separation from adjacent Figures. For a Table, it must verify the complete title, header, every row, all footnotes, and separation from adjacent visual items. Expand the verified bbox on every available side by about 8–12 pt (10 pt by default) before saving.
- After AI verification and bbox expansion, residual edge contact is not a rejection condition. Record it as `edge-contact-warning`, set `confidence: medium`, and insert the managed image block automatically.
- Mark a visual item for manual inspection only when a Figure caption-specified panel is missing; text, an axis, or a graphic is definitely truncated; a Table title, header, row, or footnote is missing; the target cannot be separated from an adjacent visual item; the caption/title is incomplete; the crop box extends outside the candidate/page and cannot be expanded safely; or AI cannot confirm completeness. Keep the complete region/page candidate, set `status: manual-review`, record both deterministic and AI reasons, and do not insert a managed image block automatically.
- Save stable Figure files as `Fig-NN.png` and Review Table files as `Table-NN.png` in `assets/<note-stem>/` beside the note.
- Save extraction metadata and failures in `figures.json`; every entry carries `kind: figure` or `kind: table` for backward-compatible manifest discovery.
- Insert managed HTML blocks immediately under matching Figure or Table headings.
- Preserve existing manual images. Replace them only when the user requests `--replace-existing`.
- Treat missing captions, implausible or out-of-bounds AI crops, duplicate image hashes, and the explicit incompleteness conditions above as failures requiring manual inspection.
- Visual extraction failure must not delete or invalidate the generated note.
